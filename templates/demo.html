How the demo works

  • Every price is a real price from this app's sources (the same ones on the dashboard), shown with its status badge and source.
  • An investment opens or closes only while the price is LIVE or DELAYED. A closed market (for example US stocks at night or at the weekend) cannot fill; the page says when it opens.
  • No fees, spread or slippage are charged. Real trading has all three, and a real order of millions of dollars would itself move the price.
  • Crypto is priced in USDT, counted as 1 USD here. Indexes, yields and other daily-only values cannot be bought (use SPY or QQQ for the stock market).
  • The chart uses live prices recorded while this page is open plus the real candles the app already has for the asset (never a stand-in "proxy" instrument).
