How the demo works

  • Every price is a real price from this app's sources, shown with its status badge and source. Charts use real candles only: crypto from Binance (streamed live into this page), other assets from the candles the app stores. 1-minute candles for non-crypto assets are built from the live prices recorded in this browser.
  • A trade opens or closes only while the price is LIVE or DELAYED. A closed market (for example US stocks at night or at the weekend) cannot fill; the page says when it opens.
  • No fees, spread or slippage are charged. Real trading has all three, and a real order of millions of dollars would itself move the price.
  • Crypto is priced in USDT, counted as 1 USD here. Indexes, yields and other daily-only values cannot be traded (use SPY or QQQ for the stock market).
  • Indicators (moving averages, Bollinger Bands, RSI) are calculated from the same real candles. They describe the past, not the future.
  • Take-profit and stop-loss are checked while a page of this demo is open.
